About Me

I am a PhD Researcher Fellow in Electronic Engineering at Kwangwoon University. My research focuses on triboelectric nanogenerators, self-powered sensors, flexible and wearable bioelectronics, functional nanomaterials, electrospun nanofibers, and digital healthcare technologies. I develop advanced materials and device structures for biomechanical energy harvesting, physiological monitoring, and human–machine interaction.

Research Interests

⚡

Triboelectric Nanogenerators

High-performance TENGs for energy harvesting and self-powered sensing.

⌚

Wearable Bioelectronics

Flexible, stretchable, breathable, and skin-compatible electronic devices.

🧪

Functional Nanomaterials

Synthesis and functionalization of MXenes, MOFs, COFs, and graphitic carbon materials.

🕸

Electrospun Nanofibers

Flexible polymer nanofibers for sensing, energy harvesting, and healthcare applications.

🩺

Digital Healthcare

Wearable systems for respiration, pulse, motion, and physiological monitoring.

🤖

Human–Machine Interaction

Self-powered interfaces, intelligent sensors, and physical artificial intelligence.
